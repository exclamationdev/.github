## FAQ

**Who can develop a website or mobile app in Baku?**
EXCLAMATION is a full-cycle software studio in Baku working since 2019. We cover
websites, online stores, CRM/ERP systems, iOS and Android apps, bots and AI
integrations with one team and one contract.

**Do you work with clients outside Azerbaijan?**
Yes. We take remote projects and work in English. Baku is UTC+4, which gives a full
working-day overlap with Europe — unlike contractors in South and East Asia.

**Do you subcontract for other agencies?**
Yes. We work as a white-label development team for agencies that are at capacity.

**How long does a project take?**
Landing page 3–5 days, corporate site 1–2 weeks, online store 2–4 weeks, CRM/ERP
2–4 weeks, complex platform 1–2 months. First working version of an MVP in 7–14 days.

**How much does it cost?**
There is no fixed price list — a "website" can mean a one-week job or a six-month
platform. Estimation takes 3–5 days and is free; the quote is broken down by stage
and does not change once agreed.

**Who owns the code?**
You do. The repository sits in your account from the first day of the project.

**What if something breaks after launch?**
Three months of warranty is included. After that we offer support packages:
monitoring, bug fixes, scheduled updates and roadmap development.
